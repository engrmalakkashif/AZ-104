# Module 30: Mock Exam and Weak-Area Remediation

**Course day:** 30 | **Focus:** Timed practice and final review

## Exam simulation
Take a full, timed practice assessment from a reputable source under exam-like conditions. Do not check answers during the attempt. Review each miss and each uncertain correct answer.

## Remediation
Classify errors, identify the two weakest domains, and return to the relevant Module-1 through Module-29 lesson entry points. Rework a lab safely or simulate it on paper. Confirm cleanup of all Azure resources and review Cost Management.

## Readiness check
Verify current skills measured, exam logistics, and policies on Microsoft's official AZ-104 page. A practice score is diagnostic, not a guarantee of exam performance.
